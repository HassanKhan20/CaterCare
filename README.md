# CaterCare

A three-sided hyperlocal home-food marketplace connecting home cooks, local buyers, and independent delivery drivers in the DFW area.

Home cooks — especially immigrants and underserved communities — list dishes and earn money cooking from home. Buyers discover authentic local food by cuisine and proximity. Drivers claim delivery jobs at transparent base pay and keep 100% of tips.

**Platform fees:** 10–12% cook commission + 8–10% buyer service fee (vs. 15–30% on DoorDash/Uber Eats/Grubhub). Built under Texas SB 541 (Food Freedom Act, effective Sept 1 2025).
